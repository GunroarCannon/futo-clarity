# FUTO Final Year Clearance Tracker 🎓📜

An authentic paper-and-ink web dossier designed to guide graduating final-year students through the 15 official clearance units, signatures, tellers, and administrative procedures at the **Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO)**.

## ✨ Features & Aesthetic

- **Tactile Paper & Ink Style**: Styled like an official Nigerian university clearance docket, featuring:
  - Wrinkled parchment paper texture overlay borrowed from Lagos Swipe.
  - Physical Manila file folder tabs and brass paperclip motif.
  - Authentic rubber stamp seals (`★ STAMP CLEARED`, `IN PROGRESS`, `PREREQUISITE SETTLED`).
  - Typewriter headers (`Special Elite`) and ballpoint pen student annotations (`Kalam`).
  - Tactile ink checkboxes, paper drop shadows, and post-it memo notes with simulated adhesive tape.
  - Subtle sound effects (paper stamp thumps, ballpoint clicks).
- **15 Structured Steps**: Organized across 4 key clearance phases:
  1. *Pay & Paper* (Clearance form, portal sundry invoices, Bursary Room 19 audit).
  2. *Campus Run* (Departmental lab, central workshop, student affairs, security part 1, registry, main library, medical centre).
  3. *Sign-offs* (Security part 2, faculty deans, accounts & records).
  4. *Finish Line* (Certificate scroll, bursary statutory release, Statement of Result).
- **Batching By Location**: Group stops by campus zones (Senate Building, Old SEET Head, FUTO Press area, etc.) to minimize unnecessary walking in the sun.
- **Bank & Budget Reconciliation**: Keep track of paid and outstanding tellers, adjust for late fees or SUG receipts, and calculate cushion allowances for miscellaneous tips.
- **Document Bag Checklist**: Automatically tallies every receipt, teller, card, and paper slip needed for upcoming stops.
- **Zero Account Required**: Automatically preserves progress in browser local storage.
