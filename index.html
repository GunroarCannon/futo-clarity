How this clearance dossier worksA 2-minute field guide before hitting campus offices

Clearance is a sequence of mandatory official sign-offs from FUTO administrative and faculty units confirming you owe zero dues, equipment, or library books. You need all stamps on your form before you can collect your Statement of Result. Departmental clearance is already settled, leaving 15 stops.

Operating Instructions

  1. Tick a step once the ink stamp lands on your paper form.Your clearance progress saves locally on this phone or browser automatically.
  2. Expand any step before heading out.Check the exact room location, documents to carry in your folder, and required fee tellers.
  3. Respect the prerequisite stops.Purchase the form, pay portal invoices, and verify at Bursary Room 19 first (Steps 1 to 3). Red tags highlight dependencies.
  4. Calibrate the Budget sheet.Turn cushion tips or late penalty switches on or off to match your exact bank receipts.

Administrative Glossary

Bank Teller
The physical paper deposit slip stamped by the bank cashier.
Remita / RRR
The electronic payment invoice. RRR is the 12-digit Remita Retrieval Reference.
Room 19
Bursary Unit verification office on the ground floor of Senate Building.
SUG Receipt
Official proof of paid Students' Union Government annual dues.
Cushion
An optional ₦1,000 allowance per unit for miscellaneous administrative tips. You can toggle this off.
Statement of Result
The final academic certificate document you collect at Old SEET Head.
